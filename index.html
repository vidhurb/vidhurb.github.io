01_PROFILE_DATA

Firmware that lives where hardware meets the OS.

I'm an embedded firmware engineer who works the full stack of a radio subsystem — from mailbox design and MAC-layer arbitration on ARM Cortex-M, up through the Android Bluetooth stack, HAL, and framework. My specialty is coexistence: the logic that lets Wi-Fi and Bluetooth share a single antenna without stepping on each other's packets.

At Samsung Semiconductor India Research, that means designing real-time coexistence state machines for Exynos SoCs, root-causing cross-layer defects with sniffer logs and GDB stack traces, and validating behaviour in anechoic chambers.

Before that, at Visteon, I built and certified the Wi-Fi/Bluetooth features that ship inside production cars — CarPlay, Android Auto, dual-phone pairing, and ASIL-compliant safety firmware. I'm most useful in the room where hardware engineers, BT stack teams, and platform software argue about whose layer owns a bug.
